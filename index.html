Helpful information

Questions before your visit?

Here are a few useful starting points. Contact the clinic if you need advice about your individual situation.

Where are 1728 Dental's clinics located?

1728 Dental lists clinics in Ang Mo Kio, Jurong East and Tampines East. See the clinic section above for addresses and contact numbers.

How can I make an appointment?

You can use the online appointment booking link or contact your preferred clinic by telephone.

What dental treatments are available?

The published service range includes dental implants, wisdom tooth extraction, emergency and surgical care, root canal treatment, Invisalign Go, orthodontics and braces, dentures, teeth whitening and scaling and polishing.

Can I use MediSave for dental treatment?

Some eligible surgical procedures may qualify for MediSave claims, subject to current rules and eligibility. Ask the clinic to confirm whether your proposed procedure qualifies and what documentation is required.

How much will my dental treatment cost?

Costs depend on your clinical needs and the treatment recommended. The clinic publishes starting prices for selected procedures. Request a consultation and confirm the full fees and applicable terms before proceeding.
