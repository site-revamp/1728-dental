# 1728 Dental Practice — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

1728 Dental Practice is a Singapore-based dental clinic group operating in Ang Mo Kio, Jurong East, and Tampines East. The practice provides affordable and accessible general and advanced dental care, including dental implants, wisdom tooth surgery, orthodontics, restorative treatments, and preventive dentistry.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the practice's affordability, treatment options, clinical expertise, and neighbourhood convenience through a clear, modern, and patient-focused digital experience.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Simplified Service Discovery

**Previous Issue:** The homepage needed a clearer and more accessible presentation of its main dental treatments, allowing visitors to identify relevant services without unnecessary navigation.

**Implemented Fix:** Organised the key treatment pathways into a structured 2 × 2 desktop grid that transitions into a single-column layout on smaller screens. This improves treatment visibility, readability, and navigation across different devices.

### 2. Stronger Trust and Pricing Communication

**Previous Issue:** Important trust-building information, including clinical leadership, the practice's patient-care approach, and treatment pricing, required greater prominence within the homepage experience.

**Implemented Fix:** Increased the visibility of clinical leadership information, patient-focused messaging, and published starting prices. Appropriate pricing qualifications accompany treatment information to support clearer expectations without implying fixed or guaranteed treatment costs.

### 3. Improved Clinic Discovery and Appointment Access

**Previous Issue:** Patients needed a more direct way to identify nearby clinics, access contact information, and proceed with appointment enquiries.

**Implemented Fix:** Introduced three dedicated clinic cards for Ang Mo Kio, Jurong East, and Tampines East, featuring telephone and directions links. A persistent two-button mobile action bar provides convenient access to clinic discovery and appointment booking throughout the mobile browsing experience.

## Design Improvements

The homepage redesign introduces several targeted visual and usability enhancements:

- **Clearer Visual Hierarchy:** Prioritised treatment discovery, pricing information, clinic locations, and appointment actions.
- **Improved Service Presentation:** Organised key dental treatments into a responsive grid for easier browsing and comparison.
- **Stronger Trust Communication:** Highlighted clinical leadership, patient-care information, and qualified starting prices.
- **More Accessible Clinic Information:** Presented the three clinic locations through dedicated cards with direct contact and directions links.
- **Mobile-Friendly Navigation:** Added persistent clinic discovery and booking actions for convenient access on smaller screens.
- **Consistent Visual Experience:** Improved content organisation, spacing, and information presentation to support a cohesive, professional homepage.

## Summary

The redesigned 1728 Dental Practice homepage addresses three primary usability and communication challenges: treatment discovery, trust and pricing transparency, and clinic accessibility.

The improvements establish a clearer, more responsive patient journey that reinforces the practice's positioning around affordable neighbourhood dental care while helping visitors explore treatments, understand available information, locate clinics, and book appointments more conveniently.
